51:1 | [title] For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. When Nathan the prophet came to him after his adultery with Bathsheba.
     | Have mercy on me, O God,   [para]
     |     according to Your loving devotion;
     | according to Your great compassion,
     |     blot out my transgressions.

     |     and cleanse me from my sin.
