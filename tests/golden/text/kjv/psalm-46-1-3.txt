46:1 | [title] To the chief Musician for the sons of Korah, A Song upon Alamoth.
     | God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.   [para]
46:2 | Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;
46:3 | Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof.
