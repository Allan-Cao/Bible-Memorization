51:1 | [title] To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came unto him, after he had gone in to Bath-sheba.
     | Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions.   [para]
51:2 | Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.
